COMMUNITY SERVICE

  • A Society Founded Upon Service

    Our members and eligibles hold a high regard for service, and recognize the value of engaging with the greater Boston community through a variety of different service events. Past service events organized by our members and eligibles have included:

    1. Habitat for Humanity
    2. Girls Who Code
    3. Challah for Hunger
    4. Relay for Life
    5. Boston Red Sox Green Team
    6. Loaves and Fishes Program
    7. Smithsonian Institution
    8. Zooniverse
    9. ZSL Instant Wild
