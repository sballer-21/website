OPEN POSITIONS

WEBMASTER
Maintain and improve the TBP website by managing our GitHub-hosted codebase, updating officer and eligibles info each semester, and pushing changes to our MIT Athena server. Great fit for someone comfortable with git/GitHub and looking to build front-end and dev-ops experience.
COMMUNITY SERVICE & TUTORING CHAIR
Lead a committee that organizes a minimum of two community service events per semester and coordinates weekly tutoring for the MIT community, while guiding eligibles through planning their own service events and review sessions. A rewarding leadership role for someone who enjoys organizing people, teaching, and giving back to the community.
ELIGIBLES CHAIR
Manage the incoming list of eligible students each year, tracking their progress through the initiation process and serving as the main point of contact for eligible-related communications and events. A great role for someone who enjoys welcoming new members and helping them get excited about joining TBP.
FELLOWSHIPS CHAIR
Publicize and run TBP's Engineering Service Fellowship program, supporting student-led projects with awards up to $3,000 during IAP and $5,000 in the summer — from info sessions and application review through interviews, logistics support, and following up on project outcomes. Ideal for someone interested in program management and helping peers turn service ideas into funded projects.
DA VINCI LECTURE CHAIR
Organize a TBP Leonardo da Vinci Lecture each semester by inviting an MIT faculty member to speak on a topic they're passionate about, publicizing the event, and providing dinner for an audience of about 30 (fully reimbursed). A great way to build a personal connection with a professor of your choice while bringing engaging talks to the TBP community.
GRADUATE STUDENT ADVISOR
A current MIT graduate student who was previously involved in TBP Massachusetts Beta or a TBP chapter elsewhere who advises the officer team, offering continuity and outside perspective as the chapter navigates decisions each year. A low-commitment way to stay connected to TBP and mentor the next generation of officers.
CORRESPONDING SECRETARY
Reach out to learn more about this role.

Reach out to the officers at tbp-officers@mit.edu if interested in one of the above positions.
